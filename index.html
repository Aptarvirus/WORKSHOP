Welcome to Hack Club!

Hack club is a global non-forpit network of hight school students who are passionate about technology and coding.
We provide a supportive community and resources for students to learn, create, and collaborate on projects related to computer science and programming. Our mission is to empower the next generation of hackers and innovators by fostering a love for technology and providing opportunities for growth and learning. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced coder, Hack Club is a place where you can connect with like-minded individuals, share your projects, and learn new skills in a fun and inclusive environment.
